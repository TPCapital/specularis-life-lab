## Resources

### Sleep Foundation

- Link: https://www.sleepfoundation.org/
- Category: Sleep / Recovery
- Use Case: Learn sleep hygiene, sleep cycles, circadian rhythm, recovery principles, and common sleep problems.
- Strengths: Accessible explanations, broad sleep topics, and practical guidance.
- Limitations: General education resource; medical issues require professional care.
- Relevance to Specularis: Useful for building a recovery system that supports learning, trading, work, and training.
- Notes: Sleep should be treated as a core performance asset, not a passive routine.
