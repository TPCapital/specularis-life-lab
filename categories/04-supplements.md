### NIH Office of Dietary Supplements

- Link: https://ods.od.nih.gov/
- Category: Supplements / Evidence-Based Reference
- Use Case: Research supplement facts, safety, dosage, interactions, and evidence quality.
- Strengths: Official health source, evidence-based fact sheets, and strong safety focus.
- Limitations: Conservative and general; may not provide personalized recommendations.
- Relevance to Specularis: Useful for evaluating supplements before adding them to a personal health system.
- Notes: Best used as a safety and evidence reference before buying or stacking supplements.
