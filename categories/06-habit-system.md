## Resources

### James Clear

- Link: https://jamesclear.com/
- Category: Habit Formation / Behavior Design
- Use Case: Learn habit formation principles, identity-based habits, environment design, and consistency systems.
- Strengths: Clear writing, practical frameworks, and strong focus on small repeatable actions.
- Limitations: Needs personal implementation; reading alone does not create behavior change.
- Relevance to Specularis: Useful for turning goals into repeatable routines and long-term compounding behavior.
- Notes: Best used to design simple systems that reduce reliance on motivation.
