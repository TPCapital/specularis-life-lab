# Specularis Life Lab

**Fitness, health, energy management, lifestyle optimization, and personal discipline.**

Specularis Life Lab is a curated research index for physical capital, fitness systems, nutrition, sleep, supplements, productivity, habit formation, and mental energy management.

This repository does not redistribute third-party content. It only links to original resources and includes personal research notes for learning, evaluation, and system design.

## Purpose

The purpose of this lab is to build a long-term life optimization archive that supports:

- Fitness and strength training
- Nutrition and body composition
- Sleep quality
- Supplement research
- Productivity systems
- Habit formation
- Mental energy management
- Personal discipline

## Structure

| Area | Focus |
|---|---|
| Fitness | Strength training, cardio, mobility, body composition, and exercise systems |
| Nutrition | Diet structure, protein intake, calories, hydration, and food quality |
| Sleep | Sleep quality, circadian rhythm, recovery, and rest routines |
| Supplements | Vitamins, minerals, omega-3, protein, magnesium, and supplement research |
| Productivity | Time management, focus systems, task execution, and workflow design |
| Habit System | Habit formation, tracking, discipline, routines, and behavior design |
| Mental Energy | Stress management, emotional regulation, recovery, and cognitive energy |
